    How to read your result

    The Disability Tax Credit isn’t based on a list of approved conditions. The CRA looks at how an impairment affects everyday activities. So instead of “yes” or “no”, each condition shows how it usually fits:

    Meets a test automaticallyBlindness and type 1 diabetes only.
    Often qualifiesFrequently approved when it affects daily life.
    May qualifyDepends on how severe the effects are.
    Unlikely on its ownMay count if severe or combined with others.
    Usually short-termNeeds to last 12+ months.

    Whatever your result, you can still apply. If you have more than one condition, check each one — limits in two or more areas can qualify together.
